# Usable source avoids reconstruction; a library is not the unique route

17 September 2026. **Accept executable-experience-retention's first publication
and close its bounded stable-use phase.** All four persistence policies complete
all eight fresh requests. Retaining or recovering source avoids model generation;
retaining the first reconstruction removes generation on subsequent requests.
This identifies avoided work under specified persistence policies, with no
observed quality advantage and no general ranking of memory formats. No follow-up
experiment is commissioned.

## What was acquired and supplied

The served Qwen3.8-27B bundle, pinned by digest, generates Python for deterministic
invoice reconciliation: paginated reads, latest event revisions, filtering,
payments/refunds, exact cents and complete sorted output. The business contract,
schemas, Python environment, five labeled development cases and dispatch to a
known routine are supplied. The acquired objects are implementation and derived
procedural prose. This demonstrates implementation acquisition from an explicit
specification, not discovery of hidden business rules or a weight update.

Every policy executes code, reads current data and checks the same development
cases. Ready source is loaded directly. Archive source is recovered directly from
a ZIP; its hash matches ready source. Lessons-only sessions receive corrected
prose, the complete contract and examples, but no source or recoverable trajectory.
Researcher logs remain outside the model's inputs and execution container. The
cache policy starts from the same lessons and retains its first reconstruction.
Thus the comparison includes competent ways to avoid forced regeneration.

Fresh seeds 8101–8108 follow development seeds 101–105. Six requests have positive
balances; two have empty results. This is one small synthetic family and one
model configuration, with no stochastic replication. All routines are requested
even for the empty customer list; a solver that short-circuits that request is
outside these policies. No contract-and-examples-only reconstruction arm is run,
so the marginal contribution of the acquired lessons is **not identified**.

## Assessment of the original EX expectations

The [original EX1–EX3](../notes/EXECUTABLE_EXPERIENCE.md) remain unchanged.

- **EX1 receives bounded support.** Available functioning source avoids measured
  reconstruction without an observed complete-quality gain. A formal skill
  library is unnecessary: archive recovery works, and reconstruction followed by
  caching pays generation once rather than on every future request.
- **EX2 is untested.** Input values vary, but neither interfaces nor obligations
  change. The pre-evaluation lesson correction is construction repair, not the
  proposed comparison of local repair with regeneration under consequential change.
- **EX3's attribution concern is addressed in part.** Equal checks and reads,
  complete paired outcomes and no extra evaluation sampling leave a clear
  avoided-generation effect. There is no paired confounded-versus-controlled
  experiment proving the predicted shrinkage. The archive and cache controls
  instead narrow which retention policies bear recurring generation cost.

## What changes in the root account

**Persistence concerns availability and subsequent policy, not registration.**
An unregistered but recoverable program remains executable experience. A policy
that begins with lessons can become a source-retaining policy after one use.
Holding it permanently in a discard regime would overstate the ongoing advantage
over a competent adaptive alternative. This sharpens the existing hypothesis
that experience can move among representations; it does not establish a learned
rule for deciding when that movement is useful.

**A functioning acquired executable is now local evidence.** Earlier evidence-use
work supplied a perfect interpreter. This study acquires a correct routine on a
different task and measures construction and reuse. It does not retrospectively
price the earlier interpreter, compare code with learned weights on one task,
or establish that acquired lessons were necessary to reconstruct this routine.

**Construction and checking belong to the retained capability.** The lesson
error was detected by an investigator using sources beyond the five checks.
Its repair cost and coverage gap matter even when all later outcomes pass.
That observation is relevant to the independent correction-lineage question,
without demonstrating the benefit of maintained dependency information. The
independent migration study still needs recipient changes; this study has none.

The [targeted public follow-up](../sources/2026-09-17-executable-retention-review/README.md)
revisits P64's sampling/selection controls and follows the ReCache lead (P85).
It distinguishes retaining source from caching model representations of schemas.
Public cache methods narrow claims about unavoidable contextual overhead; their
existence neither erases this observed generation work nor proves an untested
cached comparator's cost. No new experiment follows from the bibliography alone.

Close the bounded phase on this explanation. EX2, autonomous applicability,
changed requirements and general retention economics remain broader questions,
not automatic reasons to repeat this experiment. Procedural-memory-migration
and correction-lineage retain their independent commissions and predictions.
AD1–AD3 and EU1–EU3 retain their previous assessments.
