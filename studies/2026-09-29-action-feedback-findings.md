## What changes at the root

**Usable experience requires a capable path from evidence to action and a valid
way to judge completion.** EGI now demonstrates a competent ordinary reference
that uses history, repairs both defects and verifies its work. The learned
participant still fails to act productively. ESM advances to executable edits of
public source projects and transfers a frozen experience-fitted preference across
projects. Its strongest measured context saving on one task does not survive as a
sequence-level advantage, and executable success misses an explicit documentation
obligation. These are different failure loci, not evidence of one internal cause.

The result refines our account of co-developing models, memory and harnesses.
Making past checks executable and source copyable can make retained experience
usable. A specialist can choose among those representations. We must then ask
whether the receiving agent completes the task, whether its feedback is sound,
and whether savings survive checking and repair. A substrate alone does not
supply those properties. Neither broad EBM improvement nor its impossibility is
established by the narrow selector tested here.

## Investigation: competent ordinary reuse, unresolved acquisition

The new phase develops a shared native tool interface, copyable source/history,
an executable casebook of earlier checks, and whole-program historical reuse.
Earlier interface failures are retained. The 4B ordinary participant now completes
the strict filter repair (18/18 established checks), though its rename repair
still fails type validation (17/18). The resident 27B reference completes both
individual repairs and the joint development task.

The final joint task combines two already exposed defects: filtering all non-None
values and using `.strip()` without the required rename type validation. All three
arms receive the same source, task, tool schemas and complete eligible history.

|---|---:|---:|---|
| Learned 4B, 46 saved updates | 8/18 | 3/10 | One history search, eleven reads; no edit, probe, test or finish |
| Ordinary 4B | 17/18 | 5/10 | Repairs filter; verifies trimming but misses invalid types; incorrectly claims both repaired |
| Ordinary 27B | 18/18 | 10/10 | Repairs both; reruns relevant probes and regression suite after editing; truthful finish |

Root reproduces these grades and all 42 recorded tool actions across the three
joint arms, including final source hashes. The 27B's successful relevant checks
follow its last edit. Its result establishes feasible ordinary completion in this
instrument, not an isolated benefit of model size or casebook construction. The
interfaces and reference model changed during diagnosis without a controlled
factorial comparison.

The paired 4B arms have identical initial inputs and the same first two actions
and results. At action three the learner reads an older teacher record, while the
base reads the recent correction and subsequently acts. This locates an observed
behavioral divergence; it does not establish why the adapter causes stagnation.
The learner was stopped adaptively after twelve of an originally allowed twenty
actions; the ordinary 4B had a prospective twelve-action cap and used eleven.
The 27B used nineteen under a twenty-action cap. Do not describe this as a matched
cross-model cost comparison or a demonstrated twenty-action learner failure.

Teaching now contains 15 retained learner actions and eight executed investigator
corrections, with three defective edit targets masked while their context/feedback
remains. Root verifies all 23 action targets against their origin records. The
successful training recipe performs 46 updates and saves a 2.62 MB adapter. It
recomputes the full conditioning prefix at current weights, then detaches the KV
state and differentiates the suffix. **This is a stopped-prefix-gradient estimator,
not full-context SFT gradients.** The prefix is neither truncated nor a stale
cross-example cache. Static code inspection confirms the distinction; root does
not rerun MLX training or load the model. Estimator, targets, optimization and
learner capability remain plausible acquisition factors; none is isolated.

The preceding full-gradient attempt exceeded the requested memory guideline
(19.92 GB) and stopped after one update without a saved adapter. A cached-forward
validation attempt failed before updates; its invalid elapsed timer is disclosed.
The successful run reports 706 seconds and 5.70 GB peak MLX memory. The full phase
records 15 participant episodes, 47 total updates including the discarded one,
and approximately 72.1 minutes of conservatively charged serial heavy work.
That includes a 60-second estimate for the invalid timer, not exact device
occupancy. Investigator/correction labor remains unpriced. Its stated phase
budget was not exhausted; the stopping justification is diagnostic progress.

All results concern one authored development history. The ten new inputs test
exposed contracts, not an unfamiliar change or independent acquisition history.
The reject extension remains unevaluated. An accidentally overwritten protocol
was reconstructed from the session; the published reconstruction and hashes are
not an intact independent preregistration. These limits preserve the distinction
between a useful diagnosis and a transfer result.

**Assessment:** the lack of a functioning ordinary reference has been addressed
in this instrument. Autonomous learned acquisition remains unresolved. CC1 remains
unresolved, and CC2/CC3 remain untested. The next useful acquisition evidence would
show successful complete behavior from actual stagnant or falsely completed
states, then confirmation on fresh material. P119's learner-state aggregation
remains relevant; this recipe does not test that full method. Methods and bounded
continuation remain the investigator's responsibility under the existing remit.

## Evidence selection: real edit feedback and a qualified transfer result

Phase 6 uses pinned python-dotenv source and two earlier authored edits. Twelve
acquisition attempts include nine executable completions and failed packaging or
semantic attempts. Thirty dependent pair preferences, derived from those two
tasks, fit six linear coefficients over six candidate evidence packages. Quality
precedes cost in the preference construction. The saved fit satisfies 25/30
preferences; development selection is useful but not always cheapest.

This is experience from executed edits, rather than benchmark support labels.
Task construction, candidate packages, feedback and accepted-history selection
remain investigator supplied. The ordinary comparator uses requested symbols
and source dependencies; empirical reuse takes the earlier best completed
package by cost. Selection enumerates six proposals exactly. Calling their
negative linear score an energy does not establish a distinct EBM architecture
or an iterative energy-inference advantage.

The fixed 27B reader then attempts two later edits. Every policy completes the
`get_key` flag extension. Every policy fails the mixed-newline requirement,
despite passing the public test. Ordinary/full context fail one of six private
checks; empirical/learned selection fail three. The former uses CRLF if it occurs
anywhere, while the latter mishandles which observed ending should govern a new
assignment. The contract specifies the **first** encountered ending. Root replays
a post-hoc one-helper ordinary correction: 178 passing, one failing and one
skipped test become 179 passing and one skipped. This is an investigator diagnosis,
not an autonomous repair or new acquisition result.

The phase-6 two-task logical token totals are 59,596 ordinary, 105,130 full context,
and 52,285 each for empirical and learned selection. The latter two share identical
cached requests/responses; they are not independent successful replications.
Their lower token count comes with worse partial newline behavior and no extra
complete task. Accepted prior edits are retained; the failed newline edit is not
adopted. Task definitions already occur in the frozen transfer script: they are
held out of acquisition but were not first authored after freeze. Git history
supports the publication's chronology correction.

Phase 7 develops a self-check/explicit-finish interface on the exposed newline
task, freezes it, then authors two edits in pinned python-slugify. Git records the
new task file after comparison freeze. The learned coefficients remain unchanged;
this tests portability of an earlier preference, with no new parameter learning.

There are two task instances, not eight independent problems. The first is an
iterable-option repair; the second forwards a CLI regex option while retaining
the first. Requested docstrings are absent on the second ordinary, full-context
and learned submissions. Root inspects the requests and changed documentation,
reproducing the published post-hoc audit. Its requirement already existed; the
frozen `complete` field was an executable-test proxy. The empirical arm supplies
both artifacts but fails to finish the first within its eight-call cap. Neither
artifact completeness nor explicit finish alone captures the whole procedure.

On the first task, learned selection uses 20,657 tokens versus ordinary's 31,054
with complete artifacts. That is a useful observed saving. Verification paths
also differ: ordinary generates an incorrect case expectation, while empirical
reuse repeats an invalid fixture despite already correct production code. On the
second task the energy arm shares the full-context request path and uses 39,081
tokens versus ordinary's 24,412. Across the sequence, energy costs 4,272 tokens
more at the same measured documentation coverage. These are native logical
counts, not independent stochastic trials, deployment latency or dollar estimates.

Self-checks do not yet supply trustworthy completion evidence. The successful
development patch precedes its own generated checks; they did not cause that
repair. Root reproduces the one-helper mutation diagnostic: the ordinary scratch
suite has the same two false-expectation failures on good and defective code,
while the private mixed-newline check newly fails on the mutant. Passing other
self-tests, or producing more tests, does not demonstrate useful discrimination.
The empirical development scratch suite also misses the known newline defect
in the published audit. These observations concern specific checks, not a general
verdict against generated testing.

A post-hoc public reminder repairs ordinary's omitted documentation in one model
call (6,834 tokens), changing only the target docstring and preserving 93 passing
checks on replay. The resulting 62,300-token sequence is **review-assisted**;
it cannot be silently ranked against unassisted policies as the same treatment.
Canonical history retains the first test-passing ordinary artifact, so functional
retention does not certify documentation retention. The review repair is not
folded back into the frozen result or training record.

Across all development and comparison work, phase 6 records 41 physical reader
calls, 469,523 prompt and 11,774 completion tokens; phase 7 records 57 calls,
567,896 prompt and 20,783 completion tokens including review repair. Cache reuse
is disclosed separately. Acquisition/representation, test and repair work remain
part of the account; investigator labor and machine energy are unknown. The
phase-6 author reports three bit-identical tiny refits; root verifies their saved
artifacts and replays outcomes, but does not repeat the fits.

**Assessment:** phases 6–7 are accepted as bounded exploratory evidence with
complete-task and chronology qualifications. ES1 gains an actual edit-feedback
comparison without an established learned deployment advantage. ES2 gains narrow
cross-project use of a frozen preference, not online acquisition or general
maintenance under changing authority. ES3 gains no new iterative-inference test;
its earlier assessment remains. Expanded EBM exploration remains authorized.

## Consequences for the research program

The evidence distinguishes five statements: a tool ran; a chosen assertion
passed; external executable checks passed; all requested artifacts were delivered;
and the agent correctly verified and reported completion. Conflating them can
turn partial work into a misleading reusable lesson or training target. This is
a root inference from both publications, not a newly tested training method.

The closest complementary readings narrow the next decision. **P124, CodeT**,
uses separately generated code/test samples and execution agreement to rank
candidates; its useful reported results do not make an agent's current self-tests
an authoritative oracle. **P125, Self-Debugging**, uses prompted explanation and
execution feedback without weight updates. Its MBPP setting already asks the model
to judge correctness after a visible test passes: a serious ordinary comparator,
not a guarantee of full requirement coverage. Neither tests continuing learned
experience under changing tasks. See [versions, inspected sections and inference limits](../sources/2026-09-29-action-feedback-review/README.md#public-method-comparison).

Within existing study remits, prioritize functioning acquisition and sound
completion evidence over larger selector capacity alone. For EGI, competent
history use is now demonstrated by a reference; acquisition should address the
learner's observed action failures before interpreting fresh transfer. For ESM,
a shared way to account for explicit requirements and diagnose invalid or
uninformative checks is a justified development direction, followed by fresh
edits and full sequence costs. The investigator's proposed requirement review is
a proposal, not a demonstrated remedy or a new root commission.

Keep [intervention-choice transfer](../notes/INTERVENTION_CHOICE_STUDY.md)
independent. These studies show why remedy competence and feedback quality matter
for selecting interventions; they do not test whether intervention-choice
experience transfers after the recipient changes. Its prepared boundary remains
`3cabc29`, with no experimental publication reviewed here. Root's next synthesis
can ask when experience teaches an agent to distrust its own completion evidence,
while the ancillary investigators own concrete methods and workloads.
