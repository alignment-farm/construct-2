# Learned evidence use survives corrections, with a narrow cost result

17 September 2026. **Accept the evidence-use-under-revision publication and close
its bounded phase.** One prespecified checkpoint improves complete responses on
fresh requests and remains above the unchanged base through two corrections.
Its measured training-plus-use time is below a retained lesson's use time over
the observed sequence. Further acquisition reverses this advantage. This is
limited positive evidence for a learned-use/explicit-evidence allocation, with
neither a reliable deployment selection rule nor superiority to supplied execution.
No follow-up experiment is commissioned by acceptance.

## What was acquired and what was supplied

Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 uses a native MLX LoRA adapter trained on direct answers.
The finite task applies current JSON plan bindings and policy values to a request:
allocation, price/surcharge, lane and leftover stock. All four fields must be
correct. Every model receives the same prose operations, schema and explicit
current policy. Retrieval and authority selection are already solved by the
task. The executable reference implements those supplied operations directly.

The stable history repeats 64 requests under one policy; the varied history uses
the same requests under four policies. Each bank has 256 records. Contextual
alternatives receive an investigator-written procedural lesson and four fixed
worked examples from the available bank. They are serious alternatives, but
lesson construction is supplied and unpriced. At 128 updates the adapter has
consumed only a prefix of the bank, whereas lessons can draw on the whole bank.
No teacher reasoning calls supply the learned arm with hidden extra experience.

Fresh seed 947 changes initialization, identities and requests. Evaluation request
amounts differ from training amounts, but numeric supports overlap development.
Half the 32 cases use new entities. Independent bindings defeat the development
suffix shortcut in eight of those 16 cases. Two corrections change values and
lanes, not the schema or operation language. Frozen adapters see all three
versions: **96 use events from 32 repeated cases**, not 96 independent samples.

**EU1 has limited support.** The varied 128 checkpoint gains three complete
initial cases over base; on new entities it scores 12/16 versus 11/16, including
8/8 versus 7/8 on independently rebound cases. Base and this checkpoint both
produce valid, untruncated JSON throughout, so their quality difference is not
format validity alone. Transfer is small; allocation accuracy is initially lower
(24/32 versus 26/32), and eight zero-allocation cases per version are easy for
both. This does not establish general evidence-use competence or arithmetic
extrapolation. The stable 256 checkpoint's initial gain is confined to familiar
entities, and the varied 256 endpoint is adverse evidence for the selected
longer-acquisition recipe.

**EU2's durable benefit is observed; its relative-history explanation remains
unresolved.** Varied 128 stays +3, +7 and +3 above base. It completes 7/11 and
6/13 newly changed answers, retaining 15/16 and 14/15 previously correct unchanged
answers. It therefore loses a still-valid success at each correction. Stable
256's advantage is +5, +3 and then −2. However, no equal-update comparison gives
two clearly improved starting learners, and varied history also changes policy
diversity and exposure to later numeric values. The comparison does not isolate
“requiring current evidence” from those alternatives.

The stronger stable lesson preserves 11/11 and 14/14 of its own previously
correct unchanged answers; these are different sets from the adapter's. Across
the 96 events, varied 128 gains 18 successes and loses 14 relative to that lesson.
Its aggregate advantage does not imply casewise dominance or safer preservation.

**EU3 has a narrow observed accounting example.** Varied 128 uses 56.02 seconds
of training and 52.57 seconds of inference, 108.58 seconds from unrounded records.
The stronger lesson takes 121.16 seconds of inference for 61/96, versus 65/96.
Thus the recorded sequence covers this checkpoint's measured training cost
relative to that uncached lesson. It does not establish full deployment repayment:

- There is no shared-prefix KV cache in any arm, and the adapter emits more compact
  output. A cached lesson and a compact-output base/lesson are untested.
- Construction, checkpoint selection, failure checking and repair are not priced
  into these candidate totals. The winning checkpoint is identified from the
  assessment endpoints; there is no validated rule for selecting it in advance.
- Local timing is one observed sequence. Both 256-update endpoints cost about
  165 seconds and finish below the stronger lesson in quality.
- The supplied executable completes 96/96 in about 0.0011 seconds of recorded
  execution; its implementation is supplied, not learned or priced as acquired.

The 128 checkpoint has 38,310 training input positions and 1,741 loss positions;
its 96 uses have 27,626 prompt and 1,327 completion tokens. The stronger lesson
uses 111,626 prompt and 1,942 completion tokens. One adapter is 2,624,893 bytes.
The two shared policy revisions each edit two fields; adapters and lessons
receive no later learning. Experimental search is separate: the study reports
1,926.77 active seconds, 2,216.56 run-wall seconds and 198 reload probes in a
separate 178.69-second audit accounting. Human construction remains unmeasured.

## Explanatory progress and root selection

The bounded order diagnosis is useful: the same initialization and 256-record
multiset improve from 17/32 to 21/32 after shuffling, with all 192 shared control
responses identical. The fresh longer endpoint then regresses; it is not an
independent replication of an ordering effect. Preserve both failures. Eight
training-fit probes per checkpoint are not full-bank acquisition measurements.

The root revisited [P57, Context-Parametric Inversion, §§4.3–4.4 and 6](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10796v3).
It already shows that context-dependent training need not preserve reliance on
context, including a role for answer-prefix shortcuts. It **narrows** interpretation
of the local reversal: the trajectory is compatible with that precedent but does
not identify its mechanism. No no-context loss or matched filtering intervention
was performed here. Generic “more training can hurt evidence use” needs no new
local possibility demonstration.

This result qualifies the root's earlier absence-of-repayment summary: there is
now a favorable measured candidate comparison against an uncached contextual
lesson. There remains no full-cost advantage over the competent supplied
executable alternative, and no universal substrate ranking. Keeping values
explicit allows useful behavior to survive these corrections; it does not
guarantee reliable acquisition or preservation of that behavior.

Close on demonstrated limited transfer, durability, a measured cost boundary and
an available complete solution. Generalization, checkpoint selection and stronger
contextual controls remain open, but their uncertainty does not automatically
commission another round. Continue the independently selected
[executable-experience artifact review](../notes/EXECUTABLE_EXPERIENCE.md): the
supplied interpreter makes acquisition and persistence of implementation a
consequential distinction, without establishing an acquired-tool advantage.
AD1–AD3 retain their original wording and assessments.
