## New methods

**P120 — [Qwen-Planner-Agent, 2609.29892v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.29892v1).**
Read §2.3, selected CARE-controller details in §2.4, §2.5, §3.1's cost paragraph,
§3.2 and the opening experimental setup of §3.3. Prescribes alternating training
and harness editing, with reviewed development feedback. Its within-training
controller is a different decision problem. Reported co-evolution gains do not
isolate an experience-learned allocation policy against equal-budget fixed
alternation. The evolve set is development evidence. The quoted output-token
price excludes other substantial costs. **Narrows** a generic co-development
proposal; neither deployment autonomy nor lifetime repayment follows. No code,
checkpoints or raw outcomes were inspected.

**P121 — [MetaRSI, 2609.06396v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.06396v2).** Read
§§4.2–4.5, §5.1–5.2, §5.4 and Appendix F's accounting conventions; inspected
selected §6 discussion. Instructions retain scheduler updates from term outcomes.
The matched term-two fork reports +7.2 versus +4.9 mean points with the revised
versus original improver. This **answers** generic feasibility at the
author-reported level and **narrows** AD1 toward marginal decision value and
transfer. Meta costs have a separate ledger; root has not verified total-cost
parity or raw results. Scheduler and operator-policy changes require attribution
care. Independent transfer of acquired choice experience across recipient
families is not established by this comparison. Public implementation scope is
recorded separately below.

**P122 — [Ecdysis, 2609.11677v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.11677v2).** Read
§§3.1–3.4, §4.1, §6.2 and selected Appendix A retail/airline cases. A fixed
executor receives harness changes proposed through aggregated failures and
cross-role review. Aggregation alone increases the share of edits judged
model-specific; adding review reduces it. These labels are human judgments,
not causal identification. The authors also allow useful model-specific support.
An illustrated restriction fixes one refund error while removing a legitimate
option. **Redirects** simplistic failure-to-substrate rules toward justified
edit scope and preserved behavior. It does not compare harness and weight
remedies. Root did not replay benchmark cases or reproduce the reported outcomes.

**P123 — [ModularRSI, 2609.14857v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14857v1).** Read
§§3.1–3.7, §4, §5 and §§6.1–6.6. Promotes an earlier abstract lead. Five predefined
harness modules evolve independently before integration; a frozen function
library supports task-conditioned selection. Contrastive trajectories and edit
history inform construction. Reported comparisons support localized evolution;
the equal-aggregate-epoch comparison does not by itself establish token or
compute parity. Validation includes two sampled batch tasks, not exhaustive
semantic correctness. **Narrows** the chooser question: constructing modules,
composing them at runtime and learning adaptation allocation are different
claims. No repository, raw trajectories or benchmark outcomes were audited.
