## Inspected primary methods

**P105 — [Structured Prediction Energy Networks, ICML 2016](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/belanger16.pdf).**
Read introduction and §§2–4; selected prediction/convergence discussion. A learned
energy scores structured outputs, with approximate inference over relaxed output
variables. **Answers** generic feasibility of interaction-aware energy prediction.
**Narrows** the proposed method: optimization and rounding do not guarantee a
correct discrete output. The source supplies a mechanism, not evidence of
cross-session agent memory or an energy-specific advantage for our task.

**P106 — [Hopfield Networks is All You Need, 2008.02217v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2008.02217v3).**
Read abstract, introduction and the continuous-memory/update formulation in §2.
The associative-memory connection and relation to attention make this a relevant
alternative mechanism. **Answers** generic energy-based association; **narrows**
novelty for memory under an energy formulation. Storage/retrieval properties do
not establish correction authority, evidence sufficiency or lifecycle savings.
No author code or claimed capacity result was reproduced.

**P107 — [Energy-Based Transformers, 2507.02092v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.02092v1).**
Read §3's training/inference algorithms, §7 limitations and Appendices A.4/B.2.
Candidate optimization differs from persistent parameter learning. The authors
identify additional gradient cost, stability sensitivities and a multimodal
failure case; complementary use is also discussed. **Narrows** the idea that a
small EBM is automatically cheap or reliable. General EBT pretraining is not
selected. Public results are author-reported, without local training or inference.

**P108 — [Retrieving a Set, Not Independent Passages, 2607.05712v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05712v1).**
Read §§3–4.3 and Appendices F/G. ParaSet and SetCE give direct prior methods for
learned joint evidence selection. **Answers** the generic small set-scorer proposal;
**redirects** our comparator beyond independent ranking. Candidate-pool sizes
differ across some methods, and reported reranking latency excludes shared initial
retrieval. Annotated evidence supervision is not learner-earned feedback or a
continuing-memory experiment. Outcomes are author-reported, not reproduced.

**P109 — [The Missing Complement, 2609.20050v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20050v1).**
Read §§3–6, reproducibility statement and selected Appendices F.4–H. This is direct
prior art for state-conditioned set assembly and downstream evaluation. **Narrows**
generic sufficiency/context-reduction proposals. Released recall-complete pools
and private certificates differ from deployment discovery. The small executed-repair
cohort and AMA comparison have uncertain paired advantages; prompt reduction is
not total lifecycle cost. The paper does not establish the selected specialist's
learning across our continuing histories. Actual accessible assets are assessed
separately below; author results were not reproduced.

## Consequence for selection

The root's additional inference is that replacing a compatibility score by its
negative energy does not change set rankings. A meaningful EBM comparison must
name the differing interactions, objective or search procedure. Learned scoring,
iterative inference and learning from earlier sessions are separate claims.

Select bounded **evidence-set memory** development with a serious set-aware
alternative, useful downstream outcomes and explicit experience/update boundaries.
The study may begin with static acquisition diagnostics, but such results must not
be promoted to continuing improvement without an appropriate later-work contrast.
Keep correction authority and recoverable sources explicit. ES1–ES3 are preserved
prospective expectations; this reading supplies no supporting local experiment.
