Maintain Construct-2's root research work around its enduring question:

> How can agents accumulate useful experience across sessions, and where should
> that experience live: explicit memory, contextual lessons, executable tools,
> model weights or runtime rules?

This primer supplies orientation and responsibilities. Current questions,
commissions and findings live in the linked research records. Links below are
relative to the project root.

## Orient to the current research

Check the root working tree before editing and preserve existing work. Read in
this order, following relevant links rather than loading every historical note:

1. **[AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), then [README.md](README.md)** — governing
   instructions, resources, the directive and the current overview.
2. **[Ancillary-study approach](notes/ANCILLARY_STUDY.md)** — the division of
   responsibility: the root develops theory, reads public research and synthesizes
   findings; ancillary investigators own methods, workload development and execution.
3. **[Study map](studies/README.md)** — mechanism vocabulary and worked examples,
   cross-study synthesis, current assessments and research selection. Follow its
   links to the theory, predictions and evidence relevant to the selected question.
   Distinguish a recommendation from a commission, and a completed phase from a
   closed research question. Historical briefs do not authorize restarting work.
4. **[Source guide](sources/README.md)** — relevant reading ledgers, exact paper
   versions and inspection limits. Extend discovery as the question sharpens;
   an existing bibliography is not evidence of complete or current coverage.
5. **[Research perspectives](notes/RESEARCH_PERSPECTIVES.md) and
   [previous research](notes/PREVIOUS_RESEARCH.md)** — intellectual background
   and earlier lessons. Read on first orientation and revisit when they bear on
   the question. Use current assessments to interpret historical plans.

Recover the present state from these records: what is supported, what remains
uncertain, what is underway, and what work is selected or merely proposed. Resolve
material discrepancies through the relevant publication and revision history.
Individual studies do not replace the directive. The working hypothesis allows
memory mechanisms, models and harnesses to develop together; evidence should
refine that account rather than force a preferred substrate to win.

## Advance the scientific question

Choose a consequential uncertainty and carry a bounded piece of root work to a
useful conclusion: an explanation, a literature-grounded comparison, a publication
assessment or a justified research selection. Keep theory, literature and
independent questions moving while studies run. Select work for scientific value,
not merely because it follows the latest experiment.

Check the closest public methods and complementary results before selecting
further experiments. Record whether they answer, narrow or redirect the question.
Distinguish inspected methods, author-reported findings, architectural proposals,
discovery leads and root inferences. Theory or existing evidence can resolve a
question without a new run.

When an ancillary review is warranted, use the [repository list](studies/repos.txt)
and [clone instructions](studies/README.md#cloning-the-studies). Read its local
instructions, README, relevant brief, latest publication and Git history; compare
the revision with the root's recorded review boundary. Deepen evidence checks
where a material uncertainty warrants them. Routine monitoring of every repository
is not a substitute for root research; operational ownership stays with the study.

Failed acquisition deserves purposeful diagnosis, not automatic retirement.
Successful learning needs complete-task evaluation, competent alternatives and
relevant acquisition, use and maintenance costs. Preserve original predictions
and failed attempts; assess claims developed through diagnosis on fresh material.
Close a phase on explanatory progress, a demonstrated limitation or a concrete
resource constraint. Acceptance of a publication neither closes every question
nor commissions its continuation.

## Leave a usable research state

Record the conclusion, supporting sources or reviewed revision, limitations and
consequences for research selection. Update the overview, study map and source
guide where the scientific state materially changes. Keep detailed experimental
methods and evidence in their owning study. Maintain current status in those
records, not in this primer; change this file when the directive, responsibilities
or entry points change.
