## Question and reason to pursue it

Does periodically learning from accumulated task experience improve complete
future work beyond a competent agent that can already retrieve the same examples
and reuse the same acquired code? Does any benefit survive a change in requirements
and the cost of acquiring, checking and maintaining the adapter?

The comparison concerns the marginal value of weights inside a hybrid system.
Earlier local studies establish acquisition, limited transfer and useful external
alternatives, but do not settle this continuing-work decision. Runtime examples
are development demonstrations, with authored source corrections and unpromoted
adapters. Merely adding a hybrid arm to those tiny examples would be insufficient.

The [targeted methods review](../sources/2026-09-17-runtime-study-selection/README.md)
narrows the claim: public work already demonstrates combined memory and training,
controlled reuse streams and harness/model interactions. The proposed contribution
is a bounded allocation result under comparable evidence access, competent reuse,
fresh task composition, consequential change and measured acquisition costs.
It is not a claim to invent consolidation or hybrid agents.

## A concrete starting setting

Develop a continuing data-integration job: interpret source contracts, combine
exports and produce independently checked reconciled records. Earlier jobs leave
examples, field mappings, corrections and reusable transformation code. Later jobs
combine previously encountered operations with new source contracts; then an
authoritative requirement changes while other obligations remain valid.

Use actual integration fixtures or a motivated, openly documented analogue.
Document any synthetic construction. Development should establish why earlier
experience is useful, what transfers beyond copying an answer, and where the
executor still has consequential work to do. Deterministic parsing and arithmetic
belong in competent tools. Do not hide a supplied mapping from the explicit arm,
force code regeneration, or manufacture headroom by withholding useful evidence.
The investigator may select a better workload if this one collapses to lookup.

## The identifying comparison

At declared points in a chronological source history, fork the same available
experience and executable artifacts into two conditions:

- **External reuse:** fixed model weights with developed retrieval, retained
  examples and direct reuse of available code.
- **External reuse plus adapter:** the identical environment, context policy,
  tools and retained material, with an adapter trained from that same history.

A current-task-only reference can establish whether accumulated experience helps
at all. It is not the main opponent. Actual retrieved context may differ because
the model makes different choices; record this rather than calling it equal
exposure. Shared eligibility and access do not imply identical use.

Use fresh probes at each checkpoint for both familiar operations in new
combinations and changed requirements. Keep probe labels and outcomes out of
later training and selection. Acquire later source experience separately, under
a declared collector and feedback policy, and give it to both conditions. Label
authored or teacher corrections and charge their work. Common-history forks
isolate the additional effect of weights; they do not establish the value of an
autonomous agent choosing its own future experiences.

Choose acquisition recipes and readiness criteria on separate development
material, then preserve them for the fresh comparison. Multiple independently
constructed histories matter more than many paraphrases of a single case. The
study owns sample sizing, generation, budgets and the exact protocol.

## Expectations and informative limits

- **WC1:** Weight updates may improve reuse on fresh combinations of recurring
  operations even when source examples and code remain accessible. No incremental
  gain would favor retaining the simpler external arrangement for this setting.
- **WC2:** Learning stable behavior may survive changed current requirements;
  learning stale shortcuts may increase violations. Check complete revised tasks
  and preserved obligations, including cases where external reuse remains right.
- **WC3:** Better use-time performance need not repay acquisition and checking.
  Report cumulative complete outcomes and incurred work over the declared horizon,
  with costs in their native units and unknown engineering costs visible.

First establish a useful explicit baseline and a functioning learning treatment
on development tasks. Diagnose failed acquisition before interpreting a transfer
null. If a cheap explicit route exhausts useful headroom, or a concrete resource
constraint prevents acquisition, explain that result rather than extending the
workload until an adapter wins. A failed initial recipe alone is not closure.
