# Maintaining useful learned behavior through change

15 September 2026, after the [concurrent follow-up assessment](../studies/2026-09-15-followup-findings.md).
The user approved broadening the next experiments while keeping independent
investigations in parallel. This note records the shared direction and prospective
expectations. Local briefs commission the next phases; preparing them has not
launched experimental runs.

**Working account:** the value of an experience depends on the combined learning
plan and its later uses. A correct record can support maintenance even when it
adds no immediate answer accuracy. Correction must change the appropriate
previously learned behavior as well as generalize to new inputs. Memory must
preserve information and support the computation required by later questions.
These are connected requirements, not a claim that the studies share an internal
learning mechanism.

The larger question remains where accumulated experience should live. The next
experiments study a learned state together with its supporting evidence, write
and read methods, and maintenance costs. Their comparison is with competent
explicit memory at the level of useful behavior actually supplied.

## The next empirical milestone

Establish at least one broader workload in which useful learned behavior undergoes
recurring learning or revision, with **complete-task performance and maintenance
costs** measured against accessible evidence. For S1 and S2, this means dependent
decisions or resulting-state consequences, beyond choosing one destination in a
four-cell rule. A small sandbox workflow with prerequisites, resource eligibility
and state changes is one possible starting point. The investigator may choose a
better task; root does not prescribe its schema, model or protocol.

Score successful completion or the resulting state, with component measures to
explain failures. An emitted correct route alone is insufficient for this phase.
Use an intelligible interface and establish that the relevant operations can be
performed with the available information. Earlier routing cases can calibrate a
learner, but cannot constitute the whole new comparison. A useful learned method
need not beat explicit evidence; both useful quality and cost must be measured
before drawing a placement conclusion.

S4 broadens a different dimension: structured histories and later uses whose
relevant combinations or relations are not directly covered by writer training.
It need not adopt the procedural workload or a large language model. New histories
should exercise more than fresh draws from the same sixteen repeatedly trained
record types. Define what is unfamiliar and to whom: actual query, combination,
relation, writer training, reader training, and investigator knowledge are
different boundaries.

checkpoint pipeline or an operational dependency on one another. Each can
develop a different workload and begin bounded empirical work independently.
The already available native learning routes and S4 CPU mechanisms are starting
resources, not compulsory architectures. Investigators coordinate heavyweight
shared-device work while reading, development and analysis proceed concurrently.

## Prospective expectations

These are qualitative forecasts written before results from this phase, not
registered effect sizes or required outcomes. The root will preserve and assess
them when the new publications arrive.

| Expectation | What we expect to observe | What would change the explanation |
|---|---|---|
| **M1: anticipated damage can make selection useful.** | When an incoming update threatens a limited portion of acquired behavior, selecting rehearsal by predicted damage should preserve more complete behavior per replayed example than an untargeted mixture. | A well-developed fixed mixture remains as good after accounting for replay amount, update duration, prediction cost and future use, or predicted loss increases fail to track task damage. This weakens the proposed value of selection in that setting. |
| **M2: evidence connecting familiar and unfamiliar applications can improve revision.** | Evidence constraining familiar-instance exceptions should reduce stale behavior beyond the directly corrected examples, including withheld combinations involving familiar entities and new applications. | Only explicitly relabeled cases change, or familiar repair increases overgeneralization or collateral complete-task loss. Corrected-example recall alone is acquisition, not transfer of a revision. |
| **M3: future-use assumptions affect what survives and how it must be read.** | Once a full-evidence reader can perform the changed task, some ordinary-memory deficits will be repairable from frozen states; broader-use writing may preserve distinctions absent under narrow training. | Reader changes explain the whole deficit, or writing objectives make no useful difference with adequate acquisition and comparable resources. A failed probe alone cannot establish information absence. |

For M1, inspect existing interference-aware methods before selecting or adapting
one. A gain over the previous current-error policy is insufficient if a strong
fixed mixture achieves the same quality more cheaply. For M2, direct supervision
of familiar corrections is a disclosed intervention; evaluate transfer separately
from recall of those labels. For M3, asking for raw coordinates already used as
reconstruction targets does not supply the intended new future-use test. An
alternative reader may receive additional data or a richer answering rule; charge
and distinguish that diagnostic assistance from ordinary performance.

## S5 becomes an active measurement question

Acquisition, reuse and revision costs now belong inside these comparisons; a
separate consolidation project is not needed to begin measuring them. For a
stated number of uses and changes, compare the actual learned system, including
supporting records, against a competent explicit-evidence method. Report complete
useful performance and the costs of acquisition, verification or update prediction,
evidence retention/retrieval, rehearsal, writing, inference and any repair.

Choose a small, informative recurrence or change contrast rather than a universal
cost surface. Disclose synthetic labels, supplied rules and history-validity
decisions. A teacher or complete-rule reference may have additional information;
it is not automatically a same-information baseline. Give the explicit reference
purposeful development, just as the learned method receives acquisition
calibration. Keep training updates, tokens, wall time and storage in their own
units unless a justified conversion is supplied.

Separate the cost of executing a selected method from the investigator's full
search and counterfactual experiment. Count maintenance costs that recur during
deployment. Report quality/cost tradeoffs when methods differ in reliability;
claim acquisition-cost repayment only at comparable useful performance. More
uses between changes may favor paying an upfront learning cost, while rehearsal
and revision may erase that benefit. The experiment should determine whether
such a comparison is supported, not assume that a crossover exists.

## Execution and publication

The local investigator owns workload discovery, focused methods reading,
implementation, calibration, numerical resource sizing and routine experiments.
These commissions authorize bounded development and execution without another
routine root approval. A failed acquisition calls for purposeful diagnosis or a
functioning regime; a neural advantage is not required. Preserve unsuccessful
attempts and use fresh evaluation material for developed claims. A broader task
is valuable when it distinguishes explanations, not merely because it is harder.

Publish each phase separately with its methods, evidence, costs and identifiable
Git revision, preserving accepted publications and completed protocols. The
root compares their implications and the forecasts above. S3 remains a completed
bounded contribution; no additional depth campaign or new repository is needed
for these commissions. The existing repository list remains the inventory.
