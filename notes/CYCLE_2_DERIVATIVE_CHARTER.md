## Question and contribution

Can the fixed consumer reliably use the task's correct operation-order
convention, and how does its success depend on the representation supplied?
The development pilot's conditional oracle failed composed tasks, so a larger
lesson-construction comparison would leave an application limitation unresolved.
The contribution is a bounded diagnosis of that limitation, including evidence
that the original construction design should remain parked.

| Explanation | Informative observation |
| --- | --- |
| Extracting relations from experience is limiting | Explicit rules/table outperform raw experience |
| Applying compact conditions is limiting | The complete lookup table outperforms the conditional oracle |
| The output/execution path is limiting | Even the supplied correct current-task plan fails; saved outputs and independent validation locate participant versus harness error |

These explanations can coexist. The rule/table comparison changes presentation
and length together; it does not isolate an abstract reasoning faculty. The
reference plan is a privileged diagnostic, not learned transfer. This run has
no lesson writers and cannot establish a construction-policy advantage.

## Assigned comparison and endpoint

Follow the application protocol: 12 fresh `application-v1` worlds, seed
20260908, indices 0–11; five policies (`none`, `raw`, `oracle`, `table`,
`reference`), four tasks and two repeats per world, for **480 actor calls**.
Worlds are the independent sampled units; their tasks and repeats share a
world. Preserve the fixed model, environment, prompts, output cap, and amended
template configuration. Report per-world individual and paired outcomes,
single/composed breakdowns, costs, and counterexamples. Analysis is descriptive.

Transport failure, validator disagreement, or three consecutive empty final
outputs stops the run. No retries, replacement worlds, or outcome-driven
extension. Recovery is not currently implemented; any recovery must be
documented and tested under the protocol before use. A partial schedule earns
no admission decision, but its preserved evidence is a valid stopped return.

The existing admission criteria require reference success on **96/96 individual
tasks**, original conditional-oracle success on at least **46/48 matched pairs**
overall, and at least **22/24 composed pairs**. These are practical thresholds,
not population guarantees. Passing still requires a return here before any
larger study.
