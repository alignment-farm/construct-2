# Reusable evidence use under revision

Root research selection, 16 September 2026. The original selection below preceded
commissioning. The user subsequently commissioned the independent
[evidence-use-under-revision study](https://github.com/alignment-farm/evidence-use-under-revision)
([local brief](../../ancillary-studies/evidence-use-under-revision/README.md)).
The user reports it underway with an ancillary agent; the root launched no model run. Completed
ancillary phases remain complete.

> When experience improves an agent's use of explicit evidence, does the acquired
> behavior remain useful across successive authoritative corrections, and does
> it repay acquisition and maintenance cost against retained contextual lessons?

The enduring directive remains where useful cross-session experience should live.
The hybrid working hypothesis is already established. The issue here is which
part of experience becomes reusable behavior, which remains revisable evidence,
and when that division actually helps. “Facts outside, skills inside” is a
candidate allocation, not an established clean separation in a trained model.

The common lesson is to locate the remaining work after evidence is available.
It is not to handicap explicit access until learning wins. Retain legitimate
keys, cheap preprocessing and executable alternatives. Account for supplied
structure on both sides. In a naturally structured workload, a table may remain
the correct allocation and resolve the question immediately.

## Public work changes the question

The [dated reading ledger](../sources/2026-09-16-evidence-use/README.md) records
six versioned primary-method inspections, their author-reported results and
limits. It adds P54–P59 to the study map. No public result was reproduced here.

This literature answers the generic possibility question: learned evidence use
has positive precedents, including transfer to conflicting contextual evidence.
It also supplies a strong contextual alternative and an explanation for why
ordinary training can reduce reliance on current evidence. We therefore select
neither a new RAG recipe nor a generic counterfactual-QA demonstration.

The remaining program-level comparison concerns the useful lifetime of behavior
acquired from an agent's experience: the same inherited learner encounters
successive corrections and fresh cases while explicit evidence changes. This
differs from merely testing independent counterfactual examples against a fixed
pretrained prior. The inspected evidence does not settle cumulative maintenance
and repayment against developed contextual lessons under matched information.
This is a bounded comparison worth developing, not a certified literature gap.

## Selection among alternatives

| Candidate | Decision and reason |
|---|---|
| Another maintenance selector or richer observation model | Defer. Available actions were inadequate, and better prediction alone would not resolve that limitation. |
| Reopen AD1 retrieval-versus-reading remedy choice | Defer. The completed feasibility review found no identifying comparison in the inspected artifacts. AD1 remains open. |
| Make retrieval harder by removing visible keys | Reject as motivation. It would weaken a competent control without establishing a real need. |
| Generic learned reading or counterfactual faithfulness | Already substantially answered by public work; useful methods and controls, not the new question. |
| Reusable evidence use across corrections versus contextual lessons | Select. It connects the local supplied-operation limitation, positive public learning results and the cost of keeping accumulated experience correct. |

A motivated setting could involve an agent repeatedly applying changing service
instructions to requests: experience teaches how to locate an exception and
combine it with case evidence, while the current exception and account facts
remain explicit. This is an illustration, not a mandated synthetic workload.
Public artifacts, natural version histories or a small controlled setting may
each suffice if they distinguish the proposed explanations. Larger workloads
are not intrinsically more informative.

## Prospective expectations

These EU expectations are new, untested root predictions. AD1–AD3 retain their
original wording and existing assessments.

- **EU1 — transfer of use:** Learning from varied evidence-dependent cases can
  improve complete performance on fresh cases whose answers cannot be recovered
  from the old answers alone. I expect some transfer of evidence-use behavior;
  improvement confined to familiar answers or output formatting is insufficient.
- **EU2 — correction durability:** Experience that requires consulting current
  evidence should retain more of its benefit through corrections than experience
  that permits answer memorization. This predicts a relative difference, not
  perfect compliance or universal superiority to contextual lessons. A benefit
  that disappears at the first correction would sharply limit its useful life.
- **EU3 — conditional repayment:** Any acquisition advantage is likely to need
  repeated reuse to repay. I expect competent contextual lessons to remain
  competitive at low reuse. A learned method with neither a complete-quality
  advantage nor lower recurring cost has no demonstrated repayment case; more
  hypothetical reuse cannot rescue a method that costs more on every use.

Evidence should distinguish fresh entities or bindings from changed authoritative
answers for familiar entities. Both unchanged obligations and revised obligations
matter; an aggregate tie can conceal a damaging redistribution. Authority must
be explicit and shared: this concerns following an authorized correction, not
trusting arbitrary retrieved text or judging real-world truth from contradiction.

## Scope selected for the contribution

Recommend a bounded independent investigation of this question. Its investigator
should own workload choice, acquisition diagnosis, resource sizing and protocol.
The root's requirements are inferential: establish a functioning learned behavior,
compare current-evidence access fairly, include developed contextual lessons from
the same available experience, and assess complete useful outcomes through change.
Cheap executable solutions remain eligible where the task supports them.

An informative comparison separates learned evidence-use behavior from learning
old answers, and from simply adding teacher information, longer generation or
better retrieval. Teacher traces, if used, are additional acquired experience;
the contextual alternative should be allowed equivalent material. This does not
require a large factorial experiment: the investigator should choose the smallest
comparisons that resolve the consequential explanation.

Keep acquisition, lesson construction, evidence updates, reading, verification,
repair and inference costs visible, with experimental search separate from the
cost of deploying the selected method. Frozen reuse before further updating is
one useful way to identify durability; later repair can then reveal its price.
Neither a particular training algorithm nor a neural win is required.

The phase can close through a demonstrated durability boundary, a competent
explicit solution leaving no useful advantage, an explanation of acquisition
failure, or a concrete resource limit. Preserve failed development and use fresh
assessment material for claims developed through it. The subsequent commission prepares this question for independent ancillary
exploration. It does not restart S2/S4/S5. Methods and execution remain with the
new study; the root continues theory, literature and synthesis.
