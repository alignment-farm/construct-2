## Question and selection

When does acquiring additional evidence about a proposed reusable lesson improve
future complete work enough to justify its cost, beyond competent cheap checks,
reflection and reuse of retained source experience?

Successful execution can support an overly broad lesson; failed execution can
contain a valid useful procedure. The root's SQL review and the derivative's
separate selection/execution failures motivate inspecting the link between
episode, proposed lesson, acceptance and later use. Current runtime authority
labels assume the acceptance decision. This study investigates its evidence.
It does not reopen completed remedy, placement or consolidation phases, or
replace the program's enduring question and original AD1–AD3 expectations.

The [selection reading](../sources/2026-09-19-lesson-acceptance-selection/README.md)
deepens P48 and adds P94. Environment-probing curation and independently verified
experience already have direct precedents. The selected contribution is a
controlled comparison of acceptance evidence and its full cost, with realistic
alternatives. This is also valuable as a bounded replication with stronger
accounting; generic verification-before-memory is not a novelty claim.

The first workload lead is repeated data transformation or database reporting.
Actual source attempts can produce candidate lessons about join cardinality,
identifier normalization, missing values or conditional aggregation. A successful
source query might suggest that a display name is a safe join key while a second
source slice contains duplicates. That is an illustrative hypothesis, not a
finding or a prescribed trap. Public requirements, schema constraints and cheap
checks remain available; if they resolve the decision, record that outcome.

This lead offers executable consequences, independent numeric/plan checks and
controlled probes at modest cost. A read-only inspection or isolated small test
can challenge the proposed scope. Existing grounded fixtures are preferred where
useful, and motivated constructed episodes are permitted with explicit provenance.
The investigator chooses the workload. The derivative's all-correct authoritative
catalog is insufficient, and a broad desktop benchmark would add integration work
before resolving the acceptance question. No benchmark artifact is adopted by
this preparation.

## Comparison and prospective expectations

Use one source collector and candidate-generation procedure to supply a shared
pool before policies diverge. All conditions retain raw experience and acquired
programs. Compare raw-experience reuse, inexpensive grounded acceptance, and
acceptance with optional paid probing. Keep the primary and a competent delivery
mechanism fixed initially. Source replay and a test outside the observed source
case answer different questions; distinguish them when selecting probes.
Preserve original candidate wording and separately attribute any rewriting.

- **LA1:** When plausible candidate lessons exceed what their source episodes
  support, additional environment evidence can improve later complete work over
  inexpensive grounded checks and reflection. No incremental benefit when cheap
  checks already settle acceptance would narrow the useful role of probing.
- **LA2:** A successful replay of the source episode need not establish a lesson's
  scope. Checks that challenge an unobserved condition should better predict useful
  transfer when that condition matters; no advantage over source replay would
  limit this expectation in the selected workload.
- **LA3:** Better acceptance accuracy need not repay checking cost. Selective
  probing should be most useful when the lesson recurs, its errors are costly,
  and inexpensive checks leave a consequential uncertainty. Raw reuse or a
  developed fixed checking rule may remain preferable over the measured horizon.

Preserve these predictions in the study and append assessments. Scope validity,
acceptance frequency and verifier agreement are secondary to subsequent complete
tasks and costs. A frozen or ordinary reader isolates acceptance; it does not
settle what neural learning could add later. Training a selector or consolidator
is available when a functioning comparison earns that additional question, with
equal source opportunity and full acquisition costs.

## Commission and instrument boundary

Begin with bounded feasibility: establish useful source reuse, actual candidate
lessons, functioning checks and a consequential unresolved acceptance decision.
Then proceed autonomously to a fresh comparison when feasible. Preserve failure
and null results. An explained cheap solution can close a phase; an undeveloped
reader or a first failed acquisition recipe requires purposeful diagnosis.
