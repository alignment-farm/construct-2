## Initial question

> Can a small specialist learn from earlier experience to assemble compact,
> sufficient evidence for later complete work, and remain useful as that evidence
> changes, beyond competent ordinary retrieval and set selection?

An energy-based model is a selected candidate mechanism, not the desired verdict.
The retained archive, learned access policy and primary model can develop together.
Begin by holding the primary model fixed where this isolates the specialist's
contribution. The initial commission excluded full reasoning-model development
and reproducing a proprietary general reasoner; the subsequent user direction
above permits broader EBM exploration.

The earlier [discussion](RESEARCH_PERSPECTIVES.md) already allowed smaller
specialists. The [placement assessment](../studies/2026-09-16-placement-findings.md)
found useful score learning without useful deployment advantage. The
[runtime memory-worker assessment](../derivatives/README.md#memory-worker-review--19-september-2026)
found learned delivery below cheaper ordinary retrieval, with sensitivity to IDs
and ordering. Neither is evidence against every specialist; both require this

## Mechanism distinctions

For a task and visible state `x`, let `S` be a proposed set of source records.
A scorer `s(x, S)` and an energy `E(x, S) = -s(x, S)` induce the same ordering.
Calling the latter an EBM does not create an experimental difference. Specify
what actually differs: interactions between records, training objective, retained
state, search procedure, or their combination.

A plausible energy-based treatment learns a scalar function over a candidate
selection and uses optimization to construct a set. Continuous selection weights
can be an implementation choice; the delivered output remains actual source
records, with any rounding or discrete refinement evaluated and charged. An
informative control can search the same learned function with a different method,
or remove its interaction terms while keeping other components comparable. The
study need not run every crossing: select a contrast that separates its leading
explanations. Ordinary set scorers may themselves admit an energy interpretation.

Optimization of selection variables during a read differs from updating model
parameters between sessions. Inventory both mutable objects. A persistent source
archive can accumulate experience even with a frozen reader, while a specialist's
weights may learn a reusable access strategy. A local training result must say
which of these changed and what evidence supplied the update.

Low energy is a learned preference, not a proof of sufficiency or calibrated
confidence. Keep source identity, applicability and correction authority explicit.
An unresolved conflict may need to be delivered, rather than removed to make the
context appear coherent. Selection cannot recover evidence absent from its pool;
source discovery, selection and downstream use require separate diagnostics.

## Initial comparison and scope

Use one shared recoverable experience archive, current requirements, ordinary
tools and a fixed primary model in the initial comparison. Deliver intact records
or source-linked excerpts; opaque latent-to-LLM interfaces would introduce another
acquisition problem. Begin with a small trainable specialist over suitable
representations rather than foundation-model pretraining. Representation choice
and the location of interaction computation are study decisions, not assumptions
that embeddings preserve every required relation.

The serious alternatives are competent ordinary retrieval with deduplication and
available scope rules, a conventional learned selector, and a developed policy
that already assembles sets. They may share components. Include broad-context or
complete-evidence diagnostics when useful and feasible; these are not automatically
deployment alternatives or proofs of the correct evidence boundary. Do not deny
the ordinary policy caching, source-answer reuse or useful code to manufacture
learning value. Match available evidence and expose differences in candidate pools,
token budgets and extra search. A focused same-function comparison can identify
whether energy minimization adds anything beyond learned scoring.

The first phase includes **workload discovery and acquisition diagnosis**.
Inspect naturally motivated examples in obtainable public assets. Determine which
records interact, what the earlier experience contains, and whether complete
downstream behavior can be checked. Existing static QA or frozen-state retrieval
can establish mechanism feasibility; shuffling those rows into a stream does not
establish continuing agent experience. A motivated local adaptation is allowed,
with authored structure and its limits declared. There is no requirement to find
a workload where the EBM wins before beginning development.

The main claim needs an explicit persistence boundary and later uses whose
requirements were unavailable during the earlier update. Distinguish imported
pretraining, benchmark evidence labels, investigator-authored teaching and feedback
from the learner's own attempts. Benchmark supervision is legitimate acquisition
evidence, but cannot be described as experience autonomously earned by the agent.
Use a frozen/no-new-experience control or a targeted reset when it identifies the
contribution of accumulated selection experience.

## Prospective expectations

Preserve these statements when adding assessments. They supplement AD1–AD3 and
CC1–CC3 without rewriting those predictions or reopening their studies.

- **ES1 — Consequential complementarity.** Set interaction learning should be
  most useful when individually plausible records omit a necessary complement
  and a competent ordinary set policy still leaves errors. Its advantage should
  shrink when direct addressing or cheap scope rules already recover a sufficient
  set. Complete downstream outcomes, not retrieval-score gains alone, decide.
- **ES2 — Reusable access with revisable evidence.** A specialist trained on
  earlier experience may keep useful selection behavior when authoritative record
  values change, provided the relationships needed for the task remain applicable.
  New evidence relationships or misleading earlier feedback may remove that value.
  Test old still-valid obligations as well as new requirements, and identify which
  state was actually updated.
- **ES3 — Optimization must earn its cost.** Iterative energy minimization may
  provide no advantage over competent discrete set search or an ordinary small
  predictor. A stronger score from more search is insufficient: compare complete
  quality against actual acquisition, representation, search, delivery and repair
  work over the later use sequence. A tie at lower total work is useful evidence.

## Evidence, costs and stopping

Establish that the learner acquires its development target before interpreting
transfer. Diagnose failed acquisition through bounded comparisons that distinguish
causes; finite gradients and changed weights are mechanics only. Preserve failed
runs. Freeze the developed claim before fresh evaluation on distinct histories
or tasks appropriate to the claim; record related cases and avoid treating every
assertion or retrieved item as an independent sample.

Measure complete task behavior, omitted necessary evidence, stale or inapplicable
reuse, and the primary model's source use. Shorter context is a means, not success.
Include archive construction, labels/teacher work, encoders, indexing, learning,
iterations/restarts, retrieval fallback, primary inference and repair. Keep native
cost units and distinguish the chosen policy's operation from experimental search.
If helpful evidence arrives only after a paid controller reads the entire archive,
that work belongs in the comparison.

Stop a phase on explanatory progress, a demonstrated limitation or an actual
resource constraint. Neither an early null nor a neural win is the stopping rule.
One unavailable public package does not block independent workload development.
An explanatory ordinary solution is a legitimate result.

- **ES1: bounded support for the access condition; learned deployment advantage
  unestablished.** Ordinary dependency closure matches the learned exact policy's
  complete quote/configuration outcomes with less selection work. Neural joint
  selection improves MuSiQue annotated-support coverage, but defective evidence
  chains/labels prevent interpreting its downstream score as validated complete
  QA benefit. A serious ordinary set comparator changes the conclusion.
- **ES2: limited support with explicit maintenance.** Frozen learned coefficients
  remain useful across authored source revisions and, with exact search, longer
  native dependency chains. Scope, authority and cache invalidation are supplied.
  The study does not establish online learning, acquired authority or preservation
  of a previously mastered same-item obligation. Benchmark supervision in phase 2
  is distinct from the executed, authored feedback in phases 1 and 5.
- **ES3: supported as a local allocation constraint, not a general ranking of
  methods.** Practical gradient inference adds work and misses some good sets;
  ordinary closure or discrete beam is stronger in the tested comparisons.
  Larger search budgets repair phase-5 failures on seen material, without fresh
  confirmation. Equivalent binary rankings can have different fractional search
  behavior. Acquisition, inference and downstream correctness need separate tests.
